## SLIDE 2 — The Problem

**The marketing team is stretched thin.**

- 52 schools across 5 cities
- 3 designers, 2 PMs, 1 Creative Director
- Annual calendar: campaigns, graduations, enrollment, recruitment, anniversary, website
- It's difficult to maintain big picture strategy and long-term goals while staying on top of day-to-day operations — without additional staff and time
- Bringing in outside help at the level this work demands: significant capital investment, months of ramp-up

**We needed a better way to scale capacity without scaling headcount.**

---

## SLIDE 3 — What We Built

**An in-house AI agent team — built specifically for Uncommon Schools.**

Not generic AI tools. A coordinated team, each agent specialized, each briefed on:
- Our org structure and school network
- Our brand, audiences, and campaigns
- Our active projects and stakeholders
- Our department goals and calendar

| Role | What They Do |
|---|---|
| **Coordinator** | Oversees the team, distributes tasks, tracks pipeline, preps meeting agendas and leadership briefings |
| **Dev** | Technical architecture, engineering audits, platform strategy |
| **Writer** | Content strategy, copy, SEO, AI search optimization |
| **Creative** | Visual direction, design systems, UX, audience experience |

**The team is self-organizing.** The Coordinator oversees the other agents, assigns work, and tracks delivery. Agents coordinate directly with each other — the Dev, Writer, and Creative can work in parallel and hand off to one another without Anthony managing every step.

*You don't re-explain context. You give direction and the team executes.*

## SLIDE 5 — What We Delivered Instead

**Phase 1 equivalent — completed in days, for ~$100 in AI credits.**

**Dev (Technical Audit):**
- Diagnosed 5 fragmented WordPress installs — 5x maintenance cost, zero content sharing between sites
- Found Cloudflare CDN bypassed on ALL pages due to a UTM tracking misconfiguration — live performance failure affecting 141K monthly visitors
- Camden site running 7–10x slower than all other regional sites
- Spanish translation live but serving English content — active SEO failure
- WCAG 2.2 AA violations across the network (Title II ADA requirement)
- Recommended Drupal 10 on Pantheon: open-source, multisite, zero licensing cost, $200–400/month hosting

**Writer (Content Audit):**
- Mapped full content architecture across network + 5 regional domains
- Found all 5 sites serving a broken llms.txt file — AI search engines getting wrong information about Uncommon Schools
- Documented SEO gaps, audience pathway failures, and content strategy opportunities
- Benchmarked against peer institutions

**Creative (Experience Audit):**
- Assessed visual design, UX, navigation, and audience pathways across the full site
- Identified design direction and WCAG compliance gaps

**Lippincott's Phase 1: $125,000 — 3 to 4 weeks.**
**Uncommon Agents Phase 1: $100 in credits — 1 day.**

## SLIDE 7 — The Operating System

**Agents amplify the whole team — designers, PMs, and leadership alike.**

### For Project Management
- Every active project tracked: owner, status, deadline — near-term and long-term in one view
- Ticket writing with consistent formatting and clear scope — no more back-and-forth to clarify briefs
- Monday.com platform integration (in progress) — agents read and update the board directly (upon PM approval)
- Campaign tracking and performance analysis
- Competitor and market monitoring — ongoing, not one-time
- Region and stakeholder-specific strategy — customized context per school, per city, per audience
- **48-hour rule:** blockers flagged immediately, not in weekly reports

### For Design Execution
- Creative briefs written by the Coordinator — designers start with full context, not guesswork
- Design agents can prototype direction, generate copy, and prep specs before a designer opens a file
- Reduces revision cycles by defining scope and direction upfront
- Frees designers to focus on high-value creative work, not administrative overhead
- Management and ongoing updates to all brand guides — brand standards, video production, logo library, and photography

**The agents don't replace the PMs or designers. They remove the friction so the team can do their best work.**
